About the ICRA series

The series of ICRA conferences was established by V. Dlab in 1974. The purpose of these meetings is to exchange the latest results in an exciting research area.

From the outset it has been a major focus to bring together leading and well established experts with young researchers who are just starting out and profit greatly from the possibilities of exchange and contact provided, and from workshops where recent advances are presented in detailed exposition. There usually is organised a workshop as well as the conference itself.

ICRA Award

The Scientific Advisory Committee of ICRA 11, held in Pátzcuaro in 2004, decided to establish the International Conferences on Representations of Algebras Award.

The ICRA Award is presented at each ICRA conference for outstanding work by young mathematicians in the field of Representations of Algebras.

Aims of the award

  • to highlight important contemporary developments in the field;
  • to encourage research in representations of algebras;
  • to support young algebraists in achieving early scientific recognition;
  • to strengthen young researchers’ identification with the subject.
